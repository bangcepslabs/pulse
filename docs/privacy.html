1. Information We Process

Pulse is an information service without account registration. We only process the minimum information needed for service operation and stability.

  • Access time, request path, device and browser information, and error logs
  • Search queries, selected categories, and requests for news or market data
  • Support requests that users send directly by email

2. Why We Use This Information

  • To provide real-time news, market data, and search results
  • To monitor service stability, respond to errors, and improve performance
  • To prevent abusive requests and support security checks
  • To respond to support inquiries and operational requests

3. Retention Period

We keep information only as long as needed for the stated purposes, or as required by law.

  • Operational logs: for security and service stability monitoring
  • Support email records: for support handling and dispute resolution

4. Third-Party Services

Pulse may use external infrastructure and data providers to deliver news and market information. We do not sell personal data and do not disclose it to third parties except when required by law.
